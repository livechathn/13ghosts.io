PROTOCOL

⟨ FOUR STEPS · NO SHORTCUTS ⟩

01

Assessment

We agree on scope and permissions, review the available information and assess the nature and severity of the incident.

02

Isolation

Contain affected systems according to the response plan. Preserve relevant memory, disk and network evidence before remediation changes it.

03

Analysis

Analyse relevant artifacts, identify indicators and persistence mechanisms, and document findings alongside their limits and supporting evidence.

04

Recovery

Remove confirmed malicious components, review access and persistence, and verify recovery against the evidence available. Document remaining risks and follow-up work.
